Selected work

Real systems I built and what came from them

AI System for a U.S. Wellness Brand

Challenge: the marketing team needed to create more content and manage it across different tools.

What I did: built an AI system connecting product data, brand rules, content generation, Monday.com, Notion, and Google Drive.

Result: the system can create branded images, posters, and infographics. The project also taught me an important lesson: if people do not use an AI system, the technology alone is not enough.

AI Video Production Systems

Challenge: a U.S.-based startup needed a better way to produce AI video at scale.

What I did: built workflows connecting image generation, video models, AI voices, avatars, automation, and human review.

Result: the process became repeatable and much easier to scale.

YouTube Shorts Automation

Challenge: I needed a repeatable way to produce and publish short-form video content at scale.

What I did: built a production workflow that creates YouTube Shorts using AI image, video, and voice models.

Result: the channel reached 1,000+ subscribers with $0 spent on ads.

AI Lead Generation Agent

Challenge: finding good leads and writing personal outreach takes a lot of time.

What I did: built an AI agent that finds leads, checks them against an ideal customer profile, writes a personal message, and saves them in a CRM.

Result: research, qualification, and outreach become one automated workflow.

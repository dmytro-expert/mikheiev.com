FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of work are you looking for?

I am especially interested in Fractional Head of AI roles, long-term AI leadership work, and selected implementation projects where I can help a company move from AI ideas to working systems.

What is the difference between an AI Solutions Architect and a Fractional Head of AI?

An AI Solutions Architect focuses mainly on how the system should be designed and built. A Fractional Head of AI looks at the bigger picture: where AI can help the business, what should be built first, how different systems connect, what the team needs, and how AI projects are introduced into real work. I can do both, which means I can define the direction and also stay close to implementation.

Do you work only through Upwork?

No. Upwork is one way to work with me. You can also book a call directly if you want to discuss a role, project, or partnership.

What makes your approach different?

I do not start with a tool. I start with the problem. Then I choose the models, automation, and software that make sense for that problem.
